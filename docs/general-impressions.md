# General Impressions

## Logging into the consumer product still feels exciting
I've been using the consumer products for so long, you'd think I'd stop getting a little giddy every time I've logged into my account via web browser.

## Business trial feels like exploring a new city
Signing up for a business trial feels like walking into an airport in a new city: familiar, but oh my gosh, look at all this stuff!

## Device Trust availability thoughts
I want to check out Device Trust later. It feels like the kind of feature the homelab community would love (although I know 1Password gets a lot of flack, especially from the FOSS homelab community), especially if it was available for us at a consumer price point. I keep thinking about Tailscale providing free access to the homelab community (and others, of course!) to their enterprise-grade networking features. Something tells me that Device Trust at that scale (but tied into our paid subscriptions) would be huge!
